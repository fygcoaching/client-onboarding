Client Onboarding

Before we build your program, we need the full picture: your running background, your health history, and what's going on in your life around training. Answer everything honestly. This is a confidential document between you and your coach, not a form to impress anyone.

It takes about 10-15 minutes. Your answers save automatically on this device as you go, so you can close this and come back.

These questions are based on standard pre-exercise screening practice. Answering "yes" to any of them doesn't disqualify you from coaching, it just means we check in with your doctor first and build around it. This form does not replace the official PAR-Q+. Please also complete the current PAR-Q+ at eparmedx.com and share it with your coach before your first session.
Has a doctor ever told you that you have a heart condition or cardiovascular disease?
Do you feel chest discomfort, pain, or unusual breathlessness during physical activity?
Have you experienced dizziness, fainting, or loss of consciousness in the past 12 months?
Do you have high blood pressure that is not well controlled by medication?
Do you have diabetes or another metabolic condition (thyroid, kidney, liver)?
Do you have asthma or another lung condition?
Do you have a bone or joint problem that could be made worse by increased training?
Is there any other reason a doctor has advised you to limit or avoid increasing your physical activity?
findYOURgroove coaches movement, training load, and recovery. We do not diagnose injuries or medical conditions. If something feels sharp, localized, or doesn't resolve within 48 hours, or keeps returning, we'll point you to a physio or doctor before continuing to load it.
